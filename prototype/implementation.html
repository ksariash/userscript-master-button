A slower kind of city

On the first Sunday of every month, the market opens an hour late. The extra time belongs to the people who live above it.

A baker sets a chair outside her shop. A child draws a river in chalk. For a little while, the street feels less like a route and more like a room.

Room for an ordinary day

Residents kept the old benches and repaired the paving around them. They made room for a drinking fountain.
