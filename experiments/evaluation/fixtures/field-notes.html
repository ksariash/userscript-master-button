Notes from a community garden

Our garden began with three beds and a borrowed hose. These notes describe what we learned during its first full season.

Preparing the ground

  • Test the soil before adding fertilizer. A local laboratory can explain the result.
  • Leave paths wide enough for a wheelbarrow and for neighbors who use a mobility aid.
