Master Button key setup

This repository-owned page has no page scripts, analytics, or external resources. The installed Master Button userscript supplies the key form.

  1. Update your existing userscript to alpha.6 or later, preserving its filename.
  2. Allow Userscripts on this page, open its extension popup fully, then reload.
  3. Speech setup opens automatically. Tap Check extension storage first; it needs no key and makes no Azure request.
  4. Only after storage passes, choose your Azure resource region, paste your key, and tap Save key.

The key is saved and verified in Userscripts extension storage only. Saving also requests Azure's voice list; no article text or synthesis is sent until you request playback or export.

If no working Speech panel appears, do not enter a key elsewhere. This page itself has no key field or submission form. Physical iPhone Safari verification remains pending.

Never put a key in a URL, issue, screenshot, repository, or chat.
