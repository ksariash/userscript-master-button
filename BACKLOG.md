## Measured baseline and limits

`benchmarks/alpha6-runtime.json` is one Linux Node 24.19 run of synthetic CPU work, median of seven measured rounds (extraction three) after warm-up. It includes 50/200-paragraph inventory/extraction, 100 speech-state emissions, and 20 scroll-recovery passes. JSDOM has no layout engine. Variance/JIT mean different fixture sizes are not directly comparable; use identical workloads for before/after, and rerun paired when optimizing. No network, credentials, real inference, or Azure calls. Baseline: 200-paragraph extraction 286.636 ms, 100 speech emissions 69.582 ms, 20 scroll releases 13.149 ms. These are diagnostic measurements, not iPhone or user-visible load-time claims.

## L2: bounded preference storage

Preference reads and writes have a 3-second deadline. Failed reads never authorize overwriting unread preferences with defaults. Saving retains one active GM operation plus one coalesced latest snapshot: a deterministic burst of one active save and 100 further saves produces 2 writes, not 101. If a write times out, its result is explicitly unknown; pending callers reject and new writes are blocked until the original GM promise settles, preventing a late stale write from overtaking a newer one. Memory-only settings remain usable and the UI explains reload/retry. Key reads/writes retain their independent GM-only behavior; deletion/list/import waits are also bounded. No cancelability of GM writes is assumed.

130/130 tests pass, including hanging current/legacy reads, preservation of unread stored data, independent dummy-key save, coalescing and late-write ordering. Production/implementation preview rebuilt. The 10 ms test deadlines are injected deterministic fixtures; production uses 3 seconds for preferences and 12 seconds for key operations. An unresolved GM promise remains one retained operation until settlement or page retirement; it cannot be forcibly canceled.

## S2/L1: coalesced lifecycle and bounded nag tracking

A mutation burst has one scan timer, so continuous changes cannot indefinitely postpone inventory updates. Extraction reuses that scan instead of walking the document again. URL retirement stops timers/observers before navigation callbacks, and late asynchronous results cannot publish afterward. Extraction failures clear stale extraction evidence, retain original rows and report a retryable failure. Full source inventory scans remain necessary per batch; incremental regional reconstruction is not claimed complete.

Nag cleanup now bounds/coalesces dirty scopes (64 before a full-scan fallback), discovers shadow roots from added subtrees, caches dialog candidates while reevaluating visibility, and drops/restores detached owned nodes and roots. It drains pending mutations before testing dialog safety, preserving immediate intentional-dialog protection. Keep, controls, native dialogs and Undo regressions remain green.
